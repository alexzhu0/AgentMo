  preserved synthetic HOME is reported honestly as not discarded.

  the exact requested mode independently of ambient umask, then publishes with
  `linkat(..., AT_EMPTY_PATH)`. Failed no-replace publication closes the unnamed
  descriptor with no hidden target-root orphan and no pathname cleanup.
- Native build-output attackers must replace every named output exactly once.
  Tests now target both real `.primary` and `.verification` paths for safe-fs
  and the process supervisor.
