## Decisions Made

- Preserved D-13 admission order and retained-file read semantics: digest, decode/duplicate inspection, parse, audit, descriptor validation, and authentic result creation all use the same captured Buffer.
- Required the actual admitted value object, not merely equivalent JSON, to match the result used for provenance. This prevents a caller from laundering separately constructed data through a genuine digest record.
- Kept exact subject selection centralized and frozen. Supplying a design-plan digest without its path is unknown; supplying the path without its digest is missing required authority.
- Kept invalid/unadmitted in-memory blueprint construction available for pure validation tests, but such candidates receive no persistence brand and cannot cross the durable writer boundary.
- Kept all failure messages value-blind and left the shared secret, raw-output, host-path, and persistability policies unchanged.
