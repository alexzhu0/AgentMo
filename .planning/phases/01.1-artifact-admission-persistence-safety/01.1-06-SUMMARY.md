## Decisions Made

- Kept design contract inside blueprint. A separate `design-contract` subject would contradict the explicit one-subject `plan`/`handoff` contract and create an unplanned family.
- Returned an authentic admission result from `loadAdmittedBlueprint`, allowing handoff to prove provenance through the admission kernel's private capability rather than trusting copied metadata.
- Made `buildHandoffPackage` asynchronous so it can dynamically acquire the provenance verifier without introducing an ESM initialization cycle between handoff, admission, registry, and blueprint.
- Required the old path-only `loadBlueprint` to receive a known `pendingCommand`; exported the exact frozen owner map and added source-level tests so an eighth bypass cannot appear silently.
- Treated one read as one retained-handle capture/open, consistent with the admission kernel; bounded files may use more than one internal chunk read but are not reopened for digest, parse, or validation.
- Used the shared writer only after complete synchronous preflight. The plan claims zero side effects for unsafe candidates, not multi-file transactional atomicity after a physical filesystem failure.
