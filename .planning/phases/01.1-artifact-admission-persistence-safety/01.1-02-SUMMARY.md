## Decisions Made

- Exact carriers are distinguished from ordinary data by their closed `kind`; a recognized kind with any extra, missing, or inconsistent field is an invalid carrier rather than an ordinary object.
- `SecretPresence` requires `presentNames` and `missingNames` to form a disjoint complete partition of `allowedNames`; names are sorted, unique, uppercase, and capped at 128.
- `RedactedSummary.sha256` is the lowercase SHA-256 of its safe `text`; `redactedLength` matches that text and `empty` is internally consistent.
- Operational output paths are not treated as persisted candidate content, but candidate and exact output bytes must pass before the I/O adapter is touched.
- The I/O allowlist follows the actual later plan owners: physical writers Plans 03-09, centralized CLI output Plan 11, and maintained live-smoke script surfaces Plan 12.
