## Decisions Made

- Required every source slot to carry its own authentic admission token; a valid-looking object or an upstream `ok` field is not authority.
- Selected a strict durable domain-cases contract because reproducible domain evaluation needs exact, reviewable input bytes.
- Kept source provenance as identity, subject, and digest only; source file locations and producing-process ancestry are not durable authority.
- Preserved the separate meanings of declared readiness, isolated live execution, bounded domain quality, delivery aggregation, and production approval.
- Kept report candidates and CLI physical output as different control surfaces so Plan 10 does not claim Plan 11 work.
