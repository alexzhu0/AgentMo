<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| collector policy → provider adapter | Adapter selection and provider policy are closed, versioned inputs. |
| provider response → common record | Provider-specific metadata is untrusted and must not become evidence authority. |
| discovery records → Plan coverage | Mechanical matching and source preference must not become semantic or certification claims. |

## STRIDE Threat Register (ASVS L1; block on high)

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-07 | Tampering / Elevation of Privilege | provider metadata and declared trust | high | mitigate | Separate provider provenance, declared trust, retrieval status, evidence class, and confidence rationale; hostile overclaim tests. |
| T-03-08 | Denial of Service | GitHub pagination/rate and arXiv pacing | high | mitigate | Serial bounded pages/requests, explicit versions, stop on rate signals, no unbounded retry, aggregate collector limits. |
| T-03-09 | Information Disclosure | auth and provider payloads | high | mitigate | SecretRef/presence only, bounded header allowlist, no token/body/error payload persistence, persistability tests. |
| T-03-10 | Repudiation / legal risk | paper content handling | medium | mitigate | Metadata-first arXiv lane; links and license basis recorded; no full e-print redistribution by default. |
| T-03-11 | Spoofing | semantic coverage claims | high | mitigate | Label dedup/freshness/conflict/gap calculations as mechanical, preserve unknowns, and require later human/eval gates. |
</threat_model>
