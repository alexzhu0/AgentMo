<interfaces>
- `appendCodexUatAttemptEntry(options)` accepts a closed transition request plus an exact current-head admission; callers cannot supply sequence, predecessor, derived state, terminal status, or success.
- `loadCodexUatAttemptJournal(path)` bounded-scans one unique chain and derives current state, next legal transition, next fixed scenario, ordered evidence digest, and resumability. It maintains no mutable head file.
- Evidence, checkpoint, receipt, host observation, lifecycle result, and candidate files are immutable value-blind leaves referenced one-way by exact digest. Their schemas reject journal-head/predecessor fields and any field or digest derived from journal-head/predecessor bytes; they do not point to journal state or determine lifecycle state themselves.
- Scenario arming accepts the exact current head only as a compare-and-set precondition for the currently derived next scenario, then generates a fresh opaque correlation value independently of all journal bytes. The checkpoint successor and later observation leaf may carry that opaque value and the fixed scenario identity, but never the current head, predecessor, or a digest/encoding derived from either. The later `scenario-observed` append separately rechecks the exact current head and references the checkpoint and observation leaf digests one-way. Arming itself appends no UAT entry and proves no process/host/user/Codex origin.
- Public module namespaces expose no raw entry constructor, arbitrary append, context-authority factory, direct semantic delivery helper, or verified-host mint.
</interfaces>
