## Deviations from Plan

- **[Rule 2 - Cross-contract regression] Updated `test/artifact-subjects.test.js`.** Generated runbooks correctly end the preflight line with `&&`, but the older durable-subject test treated that control edge as part of the runtime-check argv. Its exact matcher now permits only an optional terminal `&&`; `||`, `;`, pipe, and arbitrary trailing arguments remain outside that contract and are covered by the dedicated control-flow suite.
- **[Independent review - Critical] Closed skipped-preflight path dominance.** The initial separator-aware checker still let a preflight reached through `||` authorize the next `&&` segment. An exact RED canary reproduced the bypass before the checker was changed to withhold authorization for OR-list or pipeline-entered preflights.
- **[Independent verification - Success criterion 4] Corrected helper-to-CLI flag translation.** The helper-local option had been assembled into AgentMo argv under the retired spelling. It now translates at the process boundary, with an exact positive/negative test.
- **[Evidence publication order] The planned fixed temporary filename already existed after the successful producer run.** It was not reused or overwritten; the successful receipt was validated and published exactly, preserving fail-closed new-path semantics.

**Total deviations:** 4 bounded correctness adjustments. **Impact:** independent review strengthened the same launcher/shell contract without scope or certification expansion.
